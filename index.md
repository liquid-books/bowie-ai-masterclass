# Bowie Forward

**A Microsoft Copilot Masterclass for City of Bowie, Maryland Employees**
*By Khalil Lyons*

---

## A City Built to Lead

Bowie, Maryland is not just a city — it is a statement. Founded in 1870 as a railroad junction town and incorporated as a city in 1963, Bowie has grown into the **largest municipality in Prince George's County** and one of the largest cities in Maryland, home to more than **70,000 residents**. From its origins as a railroad boomtown on the Baltimore & Potomac line to its emergence as a thriving suburban city with nationally recognized parks, public safety services, and a deep commitment to community, Bowie has always been ahead of the curve.

That same forward-leaning spirit defines this moment.

This is not a general AI overview. Every concept, every exercise, and every example in this masterclass has been built for the specific realities of **public service in Bowie** — constituent services, parks and recreation, public works, finance, public safety communications, human resources, planning, and the day-to-day work of a city government that prides itself on excellence.

By the time you complete this program, you will not just understand AI. You will be using it — every day, for real work — in ways that measurably improve how the City of Bowie serves its residents.

---

## Why Now

Two things have converged.

**First, the expectations of residents have changed.** Bowieites interact daily with AI-powered experiences in their personal lives — from banking to shopping to healthcare. They increasingly expect the same responsiveness, accuracy, and intelligence from their city government. The question is not whether the City of Bowie will modernize — it is whether it will lead or follow.

**Second, the technology is ready.** Microsoft 365 Copilot has matured from a helpful assistant into something categorically different. With the arrival of **Copilot Cowork**, city employees can now *delegate* complex, multi-step tasks — across Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and SharePoint — and receive finished, ready-to-use output. Not a draft. Not a suggestion. Finished work.

The cities that adopt these tools thoughtfully and train their people well will deliver better services, attract and retain better talent, and operate with a level of efficiency that protects taxpayers and strengthens public trust. Bowie has every reason to be one of those cities.

## The Bowie Way

Bowie's government has always reflected the values of the community it serves: **accountability, responsiveness, stewardship, and pride**. This masterclass is built around those values — because AI adoption is a culture challenge before it is a technology challenge.

| Value | What It Means With AI |
|---|---|
| **Accountability** | AI is a tool, not a decision-maker. Every output that carries the City's name is your responsibility. Verify what matters. |
| **Responsiveness** | Copilot compresses the time between a resident's need and the City's response — but the human relationship still requires your full attention. |
| **Stewardship** | Public data is the public's data. Handling it with care — understanding what Copilot can see, and what it should not — is a stewardship obligation. |
| **Pride** | AI raises the floor. Bowie employees still raise the ceiling. Use it to clear the routine so you can go deeper on the work that reflects this city's character. |
